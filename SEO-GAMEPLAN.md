## SEO Strategy Overview

### Core Approach: Topical Authority

Google rewards sites that thoroughly cover a topic. For us, the topic is **Gulf Coast beaches**. The strategy is to:

1. **Own the comparison queries** — "X vs Y beach" posts are high-intent, high-traffic, and answer exactly what travelers are Googling
2. **Own the road trip queries** — "Gulf Coast from [city]" captures people in the planning stage, primed to book accommodations
3. **Own the destination guides** — one deep guide per destination, internally linked from everything else
4. **Win the "best time / itinerary" queries** — seasonal guides and itineraries convert well and rank for long-tail keywords

### Keyword Priority Tiers

**Tier 1 — High volume, core targets** (these are the ones we need to rank for to matter)
- `gulf shores vs destin`
- `best gulf coast beaches`
- `destin florida beaches`
- `gulf coast beaches florida`
- `best time to visit gulf shores`
- `pensacola beach vs destin`

**Tier 2 — High-intent, lower competition** (these drive the most affiliate revenue)
- `gulf coast from [city]` — Atlanta, Nashville, Birmingham, Memphis, New Orleans
- `things to do in gulf shores`
- `best time to visit destin`
- `pet friendly beaches gulf coast`
- `gulf coast beach packing list`

**Tier 3 — Long-tail, high-conversion** (shelling, budget, niches)
- `best shelling beaches gulf coast`
- `gulf coast on a budget`
- `dauphin island travel guide`
- `navarre beach florida guide`
- `30a florida guide`

## Link Building Strategy

### Internal Linking Rules
- Every road trip post → links to the primary destination guide(s)
- Every "best time" post → links to the itinerary post for that destination
- Every comparison post → links to both destination guides
- Destination guides → link to all related blog posts
- Homepage → links to top 6 blog posts + all 11 destinations

### External Link Targets
When the site has more content, pursue:
1. **Travel blogs** covering the Southeast — guest post swaps
2. **Alabama/Florida tourism bureaus** — link mentions possible
3. **Local businesses** — mention a restaurant or activity → they may link back
4. **Reddit threads** (r/travel, r/Alabama, r/florida) — share genuinely helpful posts
