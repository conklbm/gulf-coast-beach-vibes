## Angle / thesis
The Wharf is Orange Beach’s “harbor village night out” — amphitheater concerts, marina lights, and a walkable strip of shops/restaurants. Treat it as an **evening destination**, not a beach day, and you’ll love it; treat it as the whole vacation and you’ll wonder where the sand went.
