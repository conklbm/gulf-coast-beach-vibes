## Angle / thesis
Gulf State Park is the AL Gulf Coast’s “do everything without leaving the park” base — 28 miles of trails, multiple beach accesses, a fishing pier, and overnight options from tents to the Hilton Lodge. Here’s how to spend a half-day or a full stay without wasting the morning in the wrong parking lot.
