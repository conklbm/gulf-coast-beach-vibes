# Gulf Coast Beach Vibes — Writer Brief

Welcome! This doc covers everything you need to write articles for Gulf Coast Beach Vibes. Read it once and you'll be set.

---

## The Site

**gulfcoastbeachvibes.com** is a Gulf Coast travel guide covering beaches from Dauphin Island, AL to Panama City Beach, FL. The voice is a knowledgeable local — not a corporate travel site. Readers are planning a trip and want honest, specific advice.

**Tone:** Warm, direct, confident. Like a friend who's been to these beaches 30 times giving you the real scoop. No fluff, no filler, no "there's something for everyone."

---

## Article Format

### Step 1 — Fill out the brief at the top of every doc

Copy this to the top of each article before you start writing:

```
TITLE:
TARGET KEYWORD: (the exact phrase someone would Google)
EXCERPT: (2 sentences max — what will the reader learn? Used as the preview on the blog index)
CATEGORY: (pick one: Travel Tips / Road Trips / Destination Guides / Comparison / Itinerary / Family Travel / Beach Life / Seasonal)
HERO IMAGE IDEA: (describe a photo that would work — beach name, time of day, vibe)
```

**Example:**
```
TITLE: 3 Days in Pensacola Beach: The Perfect Long Weekend Itinerary
TARGET KEYWORD: 3 days in pensacola beach
EXCERPT: Three days is enough to experience the best of Pensacola Beach — the snow-white sand, Fort Pickens, great seafood, and a sunset you won't forget. Here's exactly how to spend a long weekend.
CATEGORY: Itinerary
HERO IMAGE IDEA: Wide shot of Pensacola Beach at golden hour, white sand and emerald water
```

---

### Step 2 — Use heading styles (important!)

Use Google Docs' built-in heading styles from the toolbar — don't just bold text.

| Use this | For |
|---|---|
| **Heading 2** | Main sections (e.g. "Where to Stay", "Getting There") |
| **Heading 3** | Subsections within a section |
| **Normal text** | Body paragraphs |
| **Bulleted list** | Any list of items |

That's all the formatting you need to worry about. Don't use bold for section titles — use the Heading styles.

---

### Step 3 — Article length and structure

**Target length:** 1,000–1,800 words depending on topic
- Comparison posts (X vs Y): 1,200–1,600 words
- Destination guides: 1,400–1,800 words
- Road trip posts: 1,000–1,400 words
- Itineraries: 1,200–1,600 words
- Travel tips / seasonal: 1,000–1,400 words

**Every article should have:**
- An intro (no heading) — 2–3 paragraphs, hook the reader fast, state what they'll get from the article
- 4–7 H2 sections covering the topic thoroughly
- At least one "Tip" — a short callout like *"Pro tip: Book accommodations 3 months out for summer weekends"* — label it clearly with **TIP:** so it gets styled as a callout box on the site
- A brief conclusion — what's the bottom line? What should the reader do next?

---

## Voice & Style Guidelines

**Do:**
- Be specific — name actual beaches, restaurants, roads, parks
- Give real opinions — "Gulf Shores is better for families; Destin is better for couples who want nightlife"
- Use "you" — write directly to the reader
- Include practical logistics — drive times, parking, costs, what to book in advance
- Draw on local knowledge — mention things tourists miss

**Don't:**
- Don't pad — if you've said it, move on
- Don't use vague superlatives — "one of the most beautiful beaches in the world" means nothing; "the water here runs 75°F through October" means something
- Don't write a hotel ad — we recommend places to stay but we're not paid reviewers
- Don't mention specific prices that will go out of date — say "expect to pay $30–50 per person" not "$32"
- Don't use em dashes (—) excessively
- No "In conclusion" or "In summary" — just end strong

---

## What You Don't Need to Worry About

- Links — don't add any hyperlinks; those get added during publishing
- Images — don't embed images; just describe the hero image in the brief
- SEO meta tags — handled automatically
- Affiliate disclaimers — handled on the site level
- Markdown or code — write in plain Google Docs, no special formatting needed

---

## Submitting Articles

1. Write each article in its own Google Doc
2. Name the doc the same as the article title (e.g. *"3 Days in Pensacola Beach"*)
3. Drop completed docs in the shared **Gulf Coast Beach Vibes — Drafts** Google Drive folder
4. Add a comment at the top of the doc when it's ready for review

Articles will be reviewed before publishing. Feedback will come as Google Doc comments — please resolve them and re-submit rather than making a new doc.
